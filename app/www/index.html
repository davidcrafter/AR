Install AR Drawing Pro

Add it to your Home Screen — works offline like a real app.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (bottom of the screen).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The app icon appears on your Home Screen.
  4. Open it from your Home Screen — no address bar, works offline.

On iPhone this only works in Safari — not Chrome or Firefox.

Install as a real app on your phone — offline-ready.

Or open the browser menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app.
